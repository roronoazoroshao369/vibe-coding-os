# Command: Improve architecture deliberately

## When to use

Use for planned architecture improvements or repeated codebase pain.

## Required inputs

Pain points, context, tests, ADRs, constraints.

## Step-by-step behavior

1. Zoom out.
2. Identify smells and domain mismatch.
3. Propose small refactor slices.
4. Add tests/ADRs as needed.

## Outputs

Architecture review and improvement plan.

## Stopping conditions

Stop before big rewrites without approval.

## Verification checklist

Behavior protection and rollback plan are clear.

## Ghi chú tiếng Việt

Cải thiện kiến trúc theo lát nhỏ, có kiểm chứng.
