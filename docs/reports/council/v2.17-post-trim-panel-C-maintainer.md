## Summary of Findings

**v2.17 trim was effective and healthy.** The repo shed 37 skills (−24%), 6 commands, and 5 scripts with zero validation regressions. Validation time dropped from ~18s to **6.41s** (3× faster), and all 10 gates pass cleanly. No orphan scripts or stale `.mjs` imports were introduced.

**The trim left 4 release-blockers:** missing CHANGELOG entry, unbumped version, stale manifest entries, and a badly outdated `.claude-plugin/plugin.json` with 6 missing dirs and 7 duplicates. These are quick fixes (<30 min total) but meaningful for consumers.

**The underlying bus factor remains at ~1.5/5.** The repo's extensive council report library is excellent institutional memory, but there's no maintainer documentation, the release process is ad-hoc, and script categorization is absent. Adding a `MAINTAINERS.md` and auto-generating the plugin manifest would be high-leverage improvements.

**Maintenance debt is moderate and trending downward.** The trim itself reduced debt by removing entire categories of stale content. What remains is cosmetic (5 registry name mismatches) and doc-staleness (8 doc files referencing deleted commands/skills). No functional debt was found — all validators pass, all imports resolve, and the working tree is clean.

**Recommendation:** Fix the 4 release-blockers before tagging v2.17, schedule the 5 maintenance tasks for the next sprint, and invest in bus-factor improvements (MAINTAINERS.md, script categorization) as a Q3 goal.
