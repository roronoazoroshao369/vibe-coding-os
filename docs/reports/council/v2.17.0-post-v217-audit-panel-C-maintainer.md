### 5.3 Trailer Coverage

Recent commits lack trailers:

| Period | Total commits | Without trailers | Rate |
|--------|--------------|-----------------|------|
| Last 20 commits | 20 | 20 | 100% |
| Last 30 commits | 30 | 20 | 67% |

**0% trailer coverage on last 20 commits.** The provenance validator (`validate-provenance.mjs`) checks that commits on `main..HEAD` carry trailers — but since `HEAD == main`, and the provenance gate uses `main..HEAD` range, the check is vacuous. When a PR branch is used, trailers will be enforced. Currently, since all work is pushed directly to `main`, the provenance gate is a no-op.

**Recommendation:** Either adopt a policy of trailers on every commit (Signed-off-by, Co-authored-by) or adjust the provenance gate to check recent commits regardless of branch context.
